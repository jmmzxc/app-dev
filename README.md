## 📖 About the Movie

**The Amazing Spider-Man** is a 2012 superhero film that reboots the *Spider-Man* story. It follows **Peter Parker**, a shy high school student who is bitten by a genetically altered spider and gains incredible abilities. While uncovering the mystery of his parents' disappearance, he must face a dangerous scientist known as **The Lizard**.
